About us

Wealth protection for families, investors and entrepreneurs

Clermont was founded in 1996 and remains independent, owner-managed and free from private equity or bank ownership.

Our team has grown to over 150 largely professionally qualified people across Switzerland, the United Kingdom, Guernsey, the British Virgin Islands, Mauritius, Cyprus and the United States. Together they represent more than 20 nationalities and speak over 14 languages, so local technical knowledge comes with cultural understanding.

We work across jurisdictions as a single organisation. Our offices collaborate daily, so clients get the best of the whole team wherever they or we may be.

Our philosophy

Bespoke solutions

Structures designed for you, administered for life

We advise private clients, family offices and corporate bodies on designing and establishing multi-jurisdictional structures, then administer them for as long as they last, with continuity and consistency across generations.

Establishing structures

The right vehicle, in the right jurisdiction, for your family or transaction.

  • Trusts
  • Private trust companies (PTCs)
  • Corporate vehicles and transaction SPVs
  • Foundations
  • Limited partnerships
  • Syndicated investment vehicles
  • Group and regional holding companies
  • Trading companies

Advisory

Planning that looks at the whole picture, across borders.

  • Cross-border wealth, tax and succession planning
  • Analysing, advising on and structuring commercial transactions

Administration

Day-to-day running of your structures, with one team coordinating everything.

  • Full trusteeship services
  • Company board and foundation council members
  • Company secretarial services
  • Corporate governance implementation
  • Administering, monitoring and maintaining multi-jurisdictional assets
  • Coordination of external advisers and asset managers

Accounting

Clear numbers, on time, in the format you need.

  • Real-time bookkeeping and financial reporting
  • Annual financial statements
  • Bespoke financial reporting
  • Granular consolidated reporting
  • Tax and VAT returns

Regulatory and compliance

We keep your structures in step with changing rules.

  • FATCA registration and annual submissions
  • CRS categorisation and annual submissions
  • Economic substance evaluation and compliance
  • Response to legal, fiscal and regulatory developments
